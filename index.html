How to play

One box. One digit. No mouse required.

Read the rows

Every row of the sum is already on the sheet. The glowing box is your box. Type the digit that belongs there. Division answers stay whole: a quotient, plus a remainder when there is one.

Carry with Space

In multiplication, a CARRY button lights up when a carried digit needs a home. Press Space or Enter to lift it into the box above.

Ten clean to climb

Start at one digit. Ten clean rounds in a row add the next digit, up to four. The count starts again for each new digit. Only the first number grows; the second stays a single digit. A wrong digit ends the round and resets the ten count, but your digit level stays.
